> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Produce a polished Italian Medium article that reconstructs the Candy BWM 149PH7 investigation from the original physical constraint through decompilation, cloud schema discovery, protocol mapping, safety hardening, and the verified result.

**Architecture:** Build one continuous first-person narrative around evidence-driven changes of direction. Separate facts recovered from the APK, observations from the real cloud catalog, and conservative engineering decisions. Finish with transferable lessons rather than a procedural exploit guide.

**Tech Stack:** Italian Markdown, local design/specification documents, verified Python/FastAPI implementation and test reports.

## Global Constraints

- Target length: 1,800–2,400 Italian words.
- Do not publish credentials, tokens, callbacks, user identifiers, public IPs, appliance identifiers, or embedded client values.
- Describe the APK work as targeted static analysis, not complete reverse engineering.
- Do not claim official Candy support or compatibility with other models.
- State that the work concerned the owner's account and appliance.
- Keep observed data, APK-derived findings, and design decisions distinguishable.
- Preserve the verified numbers: 19 cloud records, 18 startable programs, Zoom on `OptMsk2=1`, and 172 passing tests.
- The workspace is non-Git; do not initialize a repository or create commits.
